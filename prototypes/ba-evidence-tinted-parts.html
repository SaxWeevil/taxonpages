Prototype — anatomical-part icons carry the evidence colour (PanelBiologicalAssociationsV2, Standard view)

Colour treatment

Real data: every /biological_associations/basic record for Hyperini Lacordaire, 1863 ( records, fetched ), grouped by the panel's own groupStandardAssociations() and classified by classifyStandardRow(). Icons are the PNGs from public/images/biological-associations/, embedded here.

Best wins: an icon takes the strongest mark any of its records reached — one leaf record reared from the host makes the leaf green even if four more are legacy rows. Hover an icon for its own breakdown.

The three marks, in the current treatment

Standard view — Hyperini

What this would cost

Data: groupStandardAssociations() collects parts as a flat Set and counts per row — there is no per-part breakdown today. One extra accumulation in the loop that already sees both the part and the mark would give it; nothing else changes.

Accessibility: colour becomes the only channel in the parts column. The row's aria-label and the popover that standardMarksLabel() feeds would have to move from the Records cell to the parts cell, and each icon needs its own name (“Leaf: 12 of 15 records confirmed”) — the title attributes here stand in for that.

Default view: with “show uncertain records” off, only confirmed records survive the filter, so every icon is green and the colour says nothing. The tint only earns its place once the switch is on — exactly as the dots do today.
